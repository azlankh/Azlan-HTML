My Animal Fact Card

Snow Leopard

Snow leopards are endangered big cats that live in the mountains of Central and southern Asia.

They have thick fur to keep warm in their cold mountain habitats and claws for hunting.

Fun Fact: Snow leopards can't roar like lions, but can make sounds of hissing, growls and purrs.

Giant Panda

Giant pandas are bears that live in the mountains of central China.

They have black and white fur and eat mostly bamboo.

Fun Fact: Giant pandas have a special bone in their wrist that acts like a thumb, helping them hold bamboo while eating.
