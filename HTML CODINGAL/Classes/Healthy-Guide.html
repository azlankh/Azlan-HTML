🛆 Stay Healthy

Your body is like a Supercar— and the more you use it, the better it performs. Kids who move 60 miles per day are more likely to stay healthy. Dancing, swimming, and other physical activities that gets you heart beating faster counts. Keep moving to stay healthy!

☀ Quick Healthy Tips

  • Eat a balanced diet
  • Stay hydrated
  • Get enough sleep (about 8-9 hours)
  • Exercise regularly
