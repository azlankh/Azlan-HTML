Planet Earth:Quick Guide

This is a quick guide to learning about our planet. It is filled with facts and records of our Planet.

Steps to Save our Planet

  1. Switch off unnecessary lights and electronics.
  2. Use recyclable materials everyday.
  3. Walk, cycle or use public transportation.
  4. Plant a tree in your Area.
  5. Spread Awareness about environmental issues.

Interesting Tourists Areas!

  • Amazon Rainforest: home to 10% of all species of Earth.
  • Great Barrier Reef: the world's largest coral reef system.
  • Mount Everest: the highest peak on Earth.
  • Sahara Desert: the largest hot desert in the world.

Key Terms...

Climate Change
Long-Term shifts in global temperature due to human activities.
Renewable Energy
Energy derived from natural sources that never run out.
Biodiversity
The variety of different living species in an ecosystem.
