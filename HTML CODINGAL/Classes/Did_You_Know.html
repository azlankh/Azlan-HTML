Did You Know?

1. Bananas are berries, but strawberries aren't

Botanically speaking, a berry is a fleshy fruit produced from a single ovary, which puts bananas on the list and leaves strawberries out.

2. The total weight of ants equals humans....

The world's 20 quadrillion ants weigh about the same as all people.

3. Scallops have up to 200 eyes?!

These eyes act like tiny telescopes and have their own retinas, lenses, and even a natural mirror.
